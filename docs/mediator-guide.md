# Mediator Guide

AgoraMesh mediation is voluntary, consent-based, non-custodial, and transparent.

## Before A Trade

- Disclose conflicts of interest.
- Explain your fee model.
- Explain your response time.
- Explain your rules of procedure.
- Confirm that all parties consent to your role.

## During A Dispute

- Keep evidence handling explicit.
- Do not request unnecessary personal information.
- Prefer written summaries and clear timelines.
- Do not custody funds.
- Do not pressure parties into settlement.

## Outcomes

Outcome publication is optional. Private dispute details and evidence files should remain local unless the parties intentionally share them.
